# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (sample document)

## Paid time off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly and up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year. Requests longer than 5 consecutive days must be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR.

## Expenses
Business expenses under $50 can be submitted without a receipt. Expenses of $50 or more require an itemized receipt. All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date.

## Equipment
New employees receive a laptop on their first day. Laptops are refreshed every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
