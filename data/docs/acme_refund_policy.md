# Acme Corp Refund Policy (sample document)

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the product is unused and in its original packaging. Opened software licenses are not refundable.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after the returned item is received. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

For defective items, customers can choose either a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, for up to 90 days after purchase.
